It's 3:12 A.M. and You're Rereading Every Text You Sent Tonight. Here's What Waterboy Put in a Stick to Humiliate Hangxiety.

Your body is wiped out. Your brain is running a full audit of your life. The founders of Waterboy had a name for that feeling, a fix they ran by hand, and eventually a stick for it.

WBy the Waterboy team · 5 min read

You know this one.

The room is dark. Your head is on the pillow. Your heart is going a little faster than it should. And your thumb is scrolling back through every message you sent after 10 p.m.

Did I really text that to the group chat? Why did I tell my boss's husband about my ex? Who is in the background of that story I posted? Was I the loud one? I was the loud one.

Six hours ago you were the most fun person in the bar. Now you're lying awake running a background check on yourself.

Your body is running on 10%. Your brain is convinced you ruined your life.

And it doesn't stop at sunrise. It follows you into Sunday. You skip brunch because you can't face the people who were there. You put off answering texts. You spend the day on the couch with a low hum of dread, and by Sunday night it rolls straight into the Monday scaries.

There's a name for it. People call it hangxiety: the anxious, guilty, can't-shake-it feeling the morning after drinking.

The fifth step in their routine. (No, it wasn't more water.)

Waterboy's founders, Mike and Connor, knew the feeling well. Before Waterboy existed, their morning-after routine had five steps, and one of them was aimed squarely at it. In their words:

"we'd chug down a Pedialyte to replace electrolytes + raw ginger for nausea + emergen C + l-theanine to reduce hangxiety etc."

Step five was L-theanine, an amino acid found in tea leaves. Each stick of Weekend Recovery has 200mg of it, and the number is printed on the front of the pouch. Waterboy describes its job in six words: to calm your nerves and restlessness. That's the step a glass of water or a typical sports drink leaves out, and it's the one aimed at 3 A.M.

The problem was the routine itself. Nobody wants to run five steps at 2 a.m. As the founders put it: "the last thing you want to do when you are hungover is run your own science lab and 5 step recipe."

So they put it all in one stick and called it Weekend Recovery. On their homepage they describe it in three lines: Humiliate Hangxiety. Give Nausea a Gag Order. Smack Fatigue (caffeine free).

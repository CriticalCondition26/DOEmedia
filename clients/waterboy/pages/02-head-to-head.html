What it's built for

Round 4

Taste

We'll be straight with you: Weekend Recovery tastes salty. 1,120mg of sodium is going to taste like something. A lot of people love it ("There is a perfect balance between salty and sweet."), and some people tell us it's "too salty for my taste."

The everyday stick is sweeter. If sweet is your thing, you'll like it more. Our tip if salty is new to you: mix Weekend Recovery into 24 to 32 oz instead of 16.

Winner: the everyday stick, if you want sweet. This is the round we lose.

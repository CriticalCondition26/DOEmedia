Flip Over the Electrolyte Stick in Your Cabinet. Then Read This.

We put a popular everyday stick next to Weekend Recovery, the one built for the morning after. Five rounds, label by label. We don't win all of them.

WBy the Waterboy team · 5 min read

"I was a liquid IV guy but I switched to water boy because they had less sugar and I genuinely felt better." That's a real review from one of our customers, and we hear some version of it a lot.

Here's the thing. If you already keep electrolyte sticks around, you're ahead of most people. You don't need convincing that hydration matters. The question is simpler: is the stick you buy built for the thing you're using it for?

Most everyday sticks are made for an average day. A desk, a walk, a warm afternoon. Weekend Recovery is made for one job: the night out and the morning after. So we lined them up.

Round 3

What it's built for

Waterboy's founders, Mike and Connor, used to run a five-step routine after nights out: Pedialyte for electrolytes, raw ginger, an Emergen-C and L-theanine. Weekend Recovery is that routine in one stick. Alongside the electrolytes, every stick has:

  • Ginger root extract
  • L-theanine, the amino acid found in tea
  • Vitamin C and B12, each at 500% of the daily value
  • No caffeine, so you can have one before bed

The everyday stick is a solid general-purpose drink. It just isn't built around the morning after.

Winner: Weekend Recovery, for this job.
Round 4

Taste

We'll be straight with you: Weekend Recovery tastes salty. 1,120mg of sodium is going to taste like something. A lot of people love it ("There is a perfect balance between salty and sweet."), and some people tell us it's "too salty for my taste."

The everyday stick is sweeter. If sweet is your thing, it wins this round. Our tip if salty is new to you: mix Weekend Recovery into 24 to 32 oz instead of 16.

Winner: depends on you. We'll call it a tie.

1. **No product truth sheet and no rejection ledger.** `waterboy-product-truth.md` and `waterboy-rejection-ledger.md` do not exist. Per the skill, Step F should stop here. Because the caller asked for a full review without questions, F1 was run against a proxy truth set (the label image #51, the live PDP pull, BP §4 claims lane, MS §5) and **F5 is NOT RUN**. There is no verified hangover-context review that says "water isn't enough" in those words, so the worry line should read [NO QUOTE YET] in the brief until one is found.

The only source is Waterboy's own blog post. #1 carries a HOLD; #2 (Round 1, FAQ) and #4 (item 2) carry none. BP §1 has only "30-day return ('100% Happy Endings')" from the old LP, marked [NEED: confirm]; the PDP pull shows a "Shipping & Refund Policy" link but no guarantee text. A return policy is not a money-back guarantee. Replace until confirmed.
6. **Message match to the PDP (live pull 2026-11-25).**
   - Price: PDP shows "Sitewide 20% off", sale $20.00, subscribe $17.50. Offer boxes show $24.99 struck to $19.99. Fine for evergreen after the sale ends, but recheck the day the test launches.
   - Usage: the PDP FAQ says "When do I take it? Anytime throughout the day ... first thing in the morning!" and "mix ... with 8 oz ... 12 oz" (Daily Hydration copy). 7. **"Verified buyer" labels.** `wr_reviews.json` has no verified flag per review. The PDP shows "Verified Buyer" on some reviews only. Either confirm each quoted reviewer is verified in Okendo, or change every card to "Customer review, waterboy.com."

## 2. One idea, one outcome
- **The one mechanism:** A night of drinking makes you pee out salt along with water, and plain water (or a sugary sports drink) pays back the water but barely touches the salt.
- **The one reader outcome:** Wake up after a night out with what you lost already put back, so the next day is yours.

**Four questions**
- *What:* the morning after is a payback problem, and most fixes underpay.
- *To whom:* the Weekend Regular, 21 to 40, goes out, still has a Monday (BR §3.2 A).
- *Why it matters day to day:* a lost Sunday, a slow Monday, a skipped workout, a group trip where they fade by day two.
- *Belief shift:* from "a rough morning is something you ride out with water, coffee and a breakfast sandwich" to "you lost specific stuff overnight, and you can put it back before you even wake up." **Wrong belief this page fixes:** "Water is enough."
- *Tie-in line:* Waterboy's own explainer of why alcohol makes you pee (ADH, the "hold onto water" hormone) (BR §1, waterboy.com/blogs/the-recovery-room/alcohol-dehydration). Third-party source to add: [NEED: one primary study link on alcohol, ADH/vasopressin and urine output, for the proof file].

**Top worry in the buyer's words:** "Drinking just water does not help me with my hydration" (REV 2026-07-24). Secondary: "Water alone doesn't always cut it for me" (REV 2026-09-29).

## 3. Six-part mechanism (primary)

1. **Wrong belief:** "Chug water, sleep it off, and you're fine by noon."
2. **UMP, named: "The Saturday Salt Debt."** Alcohol switches off the hormone that tells your kidneys to hold water, so you pee more, and that pee carries out sodium, potassium and magnesium too (Waterboy explainer; proof status below). By morning you owe your body water *and* salt.
3. **Symptoms it explains (buyer words):** thirsty no matter how much water you drink; "really rundown"; dragging through the next day; "Drinking just water does not help me with my hydration." (Keep to fatigue and thirst language; no nausea or anxiety promises.)
4. **Why past fixes fell short:**
   - Water: pays back the water, not the salt. (Plain and true.)
   - Classic sports drinks: built for the field, heavy on sugar. Per Waterboy's comparison chart, Gatorade 490mg electrolytes and 48g sugar (re-verify the label at publish).
   - Most electrolyte sticks: a fraction of the salt. Liquid I.V. 870mg per stick and 11g sugar per Waterboy's chart (re-verify).
   - Coffee and a greasy breakfast: they help the mood, not the debt.
   - The founders' own "5 step recipe" (Pedialyte, raw ginger, Emergen-C, L-theanine): covered more, but "the last thing you want to do when you are hungover is run your own science lab" (Our Story).
   - *It's not your fault:* nobody tells you what you lost, and most fixes were built for athletes or sick kids, not a Saturday night.
5. **UMS, named: "Bookend it."** One Weekend Recovery stick the night of (ideally while drinking) and one the next morning (Waterboy's own usage directions, LP FAQ). Each stick puts back 3,205mg electrolytes including 1,120mg sodium, with zero sugar and no caffeine.
6. **Feature links:**

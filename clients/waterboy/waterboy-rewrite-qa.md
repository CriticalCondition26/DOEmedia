### Must-fix

1. **Headline benefit (F4).**
   Current: `The Responsible Thing You Do Every Saturday Night That Still Costs You Sunday`
   Replace: `The Responsible Thing You Do Every Saturday Night That Still Costs You Sunday, and the 30-Second Habit Two Friends Use to Keep Theirs`
2. **"Half" is an unsourced number (F1). Swap all six lines.**
   - Current: `Water pays back half of what a big night takes out of you. Two friends found the other half, put it in one stick, and stopped writing off Sundays. It takes 30 seconds before bed.`
     Replace: `Water pays back the water a big night takes out of you. It doesn't pay back the salt. Two friends put the salt, and the rest of their morning-after routine, in one stick and stopped writing off Sundays. It takes 30 seconds before bed.`
   - Current: `only paid back half of what you owed.` Replace: `only paid back part of what you owed.`
   - Current: `Why water only pays back half` Replace: `Why water only pays back the water`
   - Current: `A glass of water is a five-dollar deposit on a forty-dollar overdraft.` Replace: `A glass of water pays one line of a two-line bill.`
   - Current: `and still wake up with half the bill unpaid.` Replace: `and still wake up with the salt line of the bill unpaid.`
   - Current: `See the stick that pays the other half` Replace: `See the stick that pays back the salt`
3. **Physiology line with no primary source (F1, earlier copy chief review HOLD still open).** Either add a primary study to the proof file, or:
   Current: `When you drink, alcohol turns down a hormone your body uses to hold on to water. That's why the bathroom line at the bar never ends.`
   Replace: `A night out makes you pee more. That's why the bathroom line at the bar never ends.`
4. **Subscription terms (F1, applies to all three pages).**
   Current: `Skip, change or cancel by text` Replace: `Skip or cancel by text` (unless Alex confirms "change").

### Optional

- "And it's the step most people skip." No source for "most people." Consider: `And it's the step a glass of water can't do.`
- "What we can promise is what's in the stick." With all sales final, "promise" sits close to guarantee language. Consider: `What we can show you is what's in the stick.`

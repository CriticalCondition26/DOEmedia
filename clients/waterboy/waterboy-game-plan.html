Waterboy Weekend Recovery: what the pre-sell funnel needs, piece by piece

The blind review agreed with Tommy's read: the three pre-sell pages explain a story or a spec, but none of them makes a cold reader feel what Saturday night is about to cost them. This plan re-aims each page at one desire, gives each a headline with a clear outcome, stakes and a paradox, and fixes the handoff to the product page. Every headline is a draft to test; every persona is a hypothesis.

Big idea

2. The big idea

Water pays back half of Saturday night. Bookend the night with one stick before bed and one at breakfast, and keep your Sunday.

The enemy: the responsible-feeling routine that doesn't work: chug water, sleep it off, coffee, a breakfast sandwich, and a sugary sports drink.

The mechanism: salt debt. Drinking makes you pee out sodium, potassium and magnesium with the water; water repays only the water. Weekend Recovery carries 3,205mg electrolytes, 0g sugar, no caffeine, plus the founders' old five-step kit (ginger, L-theanine, vitamin C, B12) in one stick.

The thesis: there's a better way (bookend it), two friends who lived the problem built it, and 10,497 reviewers already do it.

Desire shift: the old pages led with the founders' story and the milligram count; the proof proves the day you keep (LF3, LF1), so every page now leads with Sunday and uses the story and the numbers as proof.

Claim Defensibility Check: no competitor checked sells a two-stick routine that starts before bed (review §1). A competitor could say "electrolytes after drinking"; none can say "the five-step routine we used to run, in one stick" or own "bookend the night" first. Pass.

Validate

4. How we find out who they really are

Survey (7 questions) to recent Weekend Recovery buyers via Klaviyo, with a free Sample Pack draw as thanks: 1 what you mostly use it for (night out, trip or event, workouts, everyday); 2 how often you go out; 3 the number one thing you want from it; 4 what almost stopped you; 5 what you used before and what happened; 6 "a month from now, I'd know it was worth it if..."; 7 which of the three headlines below you'd click first. Plus ten 20-minute calls (five buyers, five who visited and didn't buy). Keep a persona if 15% or more point to her and a permissioned story exists. Quick win: add "What do you mostly use Waterboy for?" to the pop-up to tag every new subscriber.

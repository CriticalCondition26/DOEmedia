Where it started: Weekend Recovery
Sometimes we’d go out with friends and one drink would turn into many. Next thing you know, we’d wake up with a headache and a questionable IG story. Just traded 4 hours of fun for 24 hours of misery. Not a good investment. 
To recover, we’d chug down a Pedialyte to replace electrolytes + raw ginger for nausea + emergen C + l-theanine to reduce hangxiety etc. You see, the last thing you want to do when you are hungover is run your own science lab and 5 step recipe. 
So we turned it all into powder and put it inside our Weekend Recovery stick, so it’s more affordable and portable. It’s not a miracle cure, but it’s a much better way to feel less shitty for $2. 
Workout Hydration: for when you're actually trying to be a better human
Just because we like to go out, doesn’t mean we don’t value our health. High-intensity sweat sessions need way more sodium, more replenishment, and faster absorption. Most “sport” mixes? Basically flavored sugar water.
So we built

FAQ Around
 AND FIND OUT
What makes Waterboy different?
1) Our sugar-free hydration.
2) Our hyper specific functionality. No average, one-dehydration-fits-all product sold here.
3) We have 3x+ more electrolytes than our main competitor who shall not be named but rhymes with Shmiquid SmIV.
4) Our sparkling personalities.
Is Waterboy safe?
Absolutely. But for legal reasons, please consult with a doctor.
Do you ship internationally?
We do not ship internationally at the moment, but stay tuned.
When do I take it?
Anytime throughout the day you need a boost in hydration, but we recommend first thing in the morning! Or directly after your first cup of coffee!
How much water do I mix it with?
Dealer’s choice. For a more bold, salty flavor, mix Waterboy Daily with 8 oz. of water; for a lighter, breezier taste, mix Waterboy with 12 oz. of water.
Do you use any artificial sweeteners?
We like fake ingredients as much as we like fake friends, so, no. We use a small dose of organic stevia to sweeten our product, because it’s natural and linked to benefits like lowering blood sugar levels. Sorry we’re such good people.
Does it have caffeine?
Does a pope sh*t in the woods? (No. The answer is no.) Our products are proudly caffeine free.

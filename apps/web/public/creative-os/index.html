02 — STAGES

5-stage execution

Every creative pipeline in the world runs the same five stages. Most agencies use AI for stages 1-3 then fail at stages 4-5 because they try to make AI do final composition and distribution. We use deterministic tools where determinism wins.

The stages everyone gets wrong
Stages 1-3 are AI's strength. Stages 4-5 are deterministic infrastructure. Mixing them is what kills systems. AI generates fragments. Deterministic tools compose them.

06 — PRINCIPLES

10 strategy laws

Every architectural decision flows from these. The strategist agent operates by them. When something goes wrong, we trace it back to one of these being violated.

01
Taxonomies beat improvisation
Brands without taxonomies ship random content and can't diagnose. Brands with taxonomies test variations of proven categories. The strategist never invents — picks from libraries.
02
Diversity beats optimization
Single-angle libraries hit fatigue in 60 days. Healthy = 8+ of 18 angles. No single angle >60%. No single format >50%.
03
Element-level diagnosis beats whole-ad replacement
When an ad fails, only one element broke (hook/body/close/conversion). Smart strategists fix the broken element. Most operators rebuild the whole ad. Wasteful.
04
The hook earns the next 5 seconds
Hooks don't sell. They earn attention. The pitch comes later. A hook that pitches in the first 3 seconds is dead before it starts.
05
Funnel stage determines everything
Same product, three different pitches based on cold/warm/hot. Wrong stage = wrong angles, wrong formats, wrong hooks, wrong benchmarks. Lock stage first.
06
Specificity beats generality always
"Tired of being tired?" is dead. "If you're a busy mom hitting a 3pm energy wall" works. Generic placeholders = automatic rejection.
07
Strategy compounds through the Brand Brain
Every winner logged with element-level diagnosis. Every loser logged with cause. After 30-60 wins, the brand has its own playbook inside the playbook.
08
AI generates. Strategist frames. Human decides.
AI is non-deterministic and has no taste. The strategist applies frameworks. The human picks winners. Never confuse the three.
09
The cheapest iteration loop wins
Brief = $0. Generated asset = $5. Composed = $50. Launched = $500. Catch errors at the brief stage. Pre-validate everything.
10
Reference beats imagination
Every winning ad cites a real reference. Find what's working. Feed it. Generate variations. Pure-imagination prompts produce slop.
